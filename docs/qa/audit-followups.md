## Departures from the brief that the writers made on evidence (keep)

- medial-tibial-stress-syndrome: traction attributed to soleus, flexor digitorum longus and deep crural fascia (PMID 8027114, 50 cadavers; tibialis posterior attached in none), not tibialis posterior as the brief said.
- chronic-exertional-compartment-syndrome: compartment frequencies follow the surgical systematic review (PMID 27020462): anterior 51%, lateral 33%, deep posterior 13%, superficial posterior 3%.

## Evidence that is genuinely mixed and presented as such (check the labels stay)

- cauda-equina-syndrome: timing of surgery (meta-analyses favour early; a 621-patient UK cohort found no association; no validated 48-hour window).
- sacroiliac-joint-pain: no gold standard; radiofrequency evidence conflicts; the fusion RCT has device-company-affiliated authors.
- cervical-radiculopathy: collar and traction "conflicting".
- concussion, persisting-post-concussion-symptoms: exercise evidence is two adolescent RCTs from one group plus a cohort; adult guideline evidence very low to low.
- cervicogenic-dizziness: contested diagnosis (Bárány Society position); thrust manipulation "not-recommended".
- degenerative-rotator-cuff-tear, ac-joint-injury, calcific-tendinopathy: operative versus non-operative evidence mixed.
